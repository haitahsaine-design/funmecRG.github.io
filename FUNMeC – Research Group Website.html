Functional Materials for Energy Conversion Group

FUNMeC designs and studies advanced functional materials for clean energy conversion, from hydrogen production to CO₂ reduction. Our work links synthesis, characterization and device-level performance of electro- and photocatalysts.

Research topics

💧

Water splitting

Photoelectrochemical and electrochemical routes to green hydrogen, including oxide photoelectrodes and sacrificial-agent effects.

⚡

Electrocatalysis

MOF-derived, MXene and metal-oxide catalysts for hydrogen evolution, oxygen evolution and CO₂ reduction.

☀️

Photocatalysis

Vanadate and BiVO₄-based photocatalysts for pollutant degradation under light.
